# Surface brief: Voltaic Cars site (all five pages)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: course reviewer role-playing a Spanish buyer of a premium EV. Action: reservar test drive. Proof: three models with specs and prices, studio photography. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, keep existing behaviours.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every Voltaic is presented as a run in the wind tunnel: air draws the car, the data is logged on the image. Refuses the dark-gradient-glow EV template with centered headings and icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Matte tunnel black #0c0d0f, graphite #17191c, smoke white #e9e6df, test-lamp amber #ff8a1f as the only action colour, car blue only inside photos. Archivo expanded heavy for full-measure headlines, Geist body, Martian Mono only for measurements and run log data. Hairline 1px smoke rules, square corners, no shadows, glows or gradients; depth only by overlap of flat layers (photo, smoke, type). Annotations are leader lines pinned to points on the car.

STORY: The visitor sees the car under test, reads three numbers pinned to it, understands the line-up as three logged runs, compares them in a technical table, and books a test drive (a run of their own).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Mark 3 photograph, canvas smoke streamlines flowing left to right over it and bending around the cursor. Headline in Archivo expanded spanning the full width at the bottom. Three leader-line annotations (650 km, 3,2 s, 650 CV) pinned to the car. Amber "Reservar test drive" button bottom right, secondary text link "Ver los modelos".

FORM: Wind tunnel smoke-line test record; position 7 on the ordered list; seed key 373d8dc6. Signature interaction: cursor-deflected smoke streamlines in the hero; motion grammar: one continuous smoke line down the page as scroll progress, annotations drawing in once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
