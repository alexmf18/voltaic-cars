# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Visitors of a course/portfolio project: instructors, peers and recruiters evaluating the craft, role-playing a Spanish-speaking buyer considering a premium electric car.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Voltaic Cars, a fictional European maker of high-performance electric cars. Five pages: Inicio, Modelos, Precios, Galería, Contacto. Success: the site feels like a credible, distinctive car brand and pushes toward booking a test drive.

## Positioning
Three models with clear roles: Mark 1 (sedán deportivo, 42.990€), Mark 2 (SUV familiar, 52.490€), Mark 3 (Gran Turismo flagship, 68.990€). Performance plus everyday practicality, European engineering.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step. Existing behaviour to preserve: mobile nav, scroll reveal, counters, gallery lightbox, validated contact form, back-to-top. Spanish copy (lang="es").

## Brand Commitments
Name "Voltaic Cars" and the lightning-bolt idea. Logo colours may be reinterpreted. Copy may be rewritten; models, specs and prices stay.

## Evidence on Hand
Photography: presentacion.png, presentacion2.png, presentacion (2).png, mark1/2/3.png (+_min), concesionario.jpg — blue cars in a dark studio with vertical amber light bars. All data is fictional (course project).

## Product Principles
- The cars and their numbers are the hero, not decoration.
- One clear action: book a test drive.
- Distinctive over generic "EV startup" look.
